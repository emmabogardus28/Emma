## The thing to remember
This is the only property outside Arizona.

**Hawaii is 3 hours behind Arizona, all year.** Neither state observes daylight saving, so the gap never changes. 9:00 AM in Scottsdale is 6:00 AM in Kona. Do not schedule a call or expect a same-morning reply on Hawaii time without checking the clock first. (Time zone math from CC, not a fact about the property.)
