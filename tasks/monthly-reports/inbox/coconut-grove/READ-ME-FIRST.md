## Why this property cannot use the same process as the others

Every other property in the portfolio (731 Goodyear, 801 Greenfield Gateway, 802 Bell Medical
Center, 803 Roosevelt Commons) runs on the same six line Executive Summary. **Coconut Grove
does not fit it.**

**It is reported on an accrual basis.** The header says "Accrual". Every other property says
"Cash". That is a different accounting basis, not a formatting difference.

**It has three profit lines the others do not have**, because it carries corporate overhead
and a ground lease:

| Line | July MTD actual | July MTD budget |
|---|---|---|
| Total Income | $349,463.18 | $340,807.00 |
| Total Common Area Expense | $133,031.77 | $128,388.91 |
| **Net Operating Income** | $216,431.41 | $212,418.09 |
| Total Owner Expenses | $250,837.22 | $5,025.00 |
| **Adjusted Net Operating Income** | ($34,405.81) | $207,393.09 |
| Total Corporate G&A Expenses | $10,629.09 | $18,458.00 |
| **Adjusted NOI after G&A** | ($45,034.90) | $188,935.09 |
| Total Interest Expense | $145,075.09 | $160,833.00 |
| **Net Income** | **($190,109.99)** | **$28,102.09** |

Checked the arithmetic all the way down, month and year to date. Every step ties.

The six line template has no row for Corporate G&A and no Adjusted NOI. Dropping those two
lines to force it into the standard form would misstate the property.

**It also has income and expense types the others do not:** percentage rent, parking rent,
food court CAM, food court janitorial, food court gas, food court security, food court trash,
marketing income, ground rent, general excise tax (Hawaii, not sales tax), a Morgan Stanley
interest line and a separate swap interest line.

**And it reports tenant gross sales**, which none of the others do. Athena's procedure has a
step for Gross Sales comments and this is the property it is written for.

## The thing somebody needs to look at

**July net income missed budget by $218,212.08.** One line explains almost all of it:

> `70463-000000 - LOSS AGAINST INS CLAIMS` **$239,946.60** booked in July against a zero budget.

Year to date that same account sits at **($157,053.51)**, a credit, so something was booked
the other way earlier in the year and reversed or recovered. Without July's charge the month
would have come in at roughly **$49,836.61**, comfortably ahead of the $28,102.09 budget.

There is also a **$300,000.00 movement on `12100 LOAN RECEIVABLE`** in July, shown under
capital expenses.

Neither is explained anywhere in the files here. Both are large and both need an answer before
anything goes to the owner.
